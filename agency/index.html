FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Are the Google reviews real?

Yes, every one. Each review comes from a real customer of yours. We make it easy for them to leave one; we never write, buy or pay for reviews, which Google prohibits.

What if someone leaves a bad review?

It happens to every business. We alert you, help you write a calm, professional reply, and help you fix what caused it. A few honest critiques among many good reviews make your profile more believable, not less.

How fast will I see results?

Review invitations usually bring new reviews within the first couple of weeks. Local SEO builds over a few months, and ads can drive calls from the day they go live.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month. We’d rather keep you because it’s working.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local and service businesses of all kinds: restaurants, salons, clinics, trades, retail, fitness and more. If customers search for you on Google, we can help.

How much of my time will this take?

About an hour to get started, then a short monthly check-in. We handle the rest.
