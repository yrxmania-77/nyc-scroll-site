FAQ

Common questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How much does it cost?

Our standard rate is $10 per confirmed live review. You are only charged for reviews that are verified as live.

How do customers leave a review?

Our system generates QR codes for your business. A customer scans the code and goes straight to your Google review page to share their experience.

How do I know which reviews went live?

We track reviews through our dashboard, and every Sunday we prepare a report showing which reviews went live and remained posted.

What if a review doesn’t go live?

Then you are not charged for it. Billing covers verified live reviews only, and the Sunday report shows each review’s status.

Are the reviews from real customers?

Yes. Our goal is to help businesses build a stronger online reputation through genuine customer feedback from the people they actually serve.

How many businesses do you work with?

Over 500 businesses, supported by hundreds of workers across different locations.
