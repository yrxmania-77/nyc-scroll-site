About the journey

Scroll, and the map gives way. Each place arrives by descent: down out of the aerial city, into the street, then a release that pulls you back up to where you started. Four descents, one hub, entirely under your control.

01 · Central Park · Manhattan

Central Park

An 843-acre green heart in Manhattan — lakes, meadows, and paths ringed by skyline.

Fact Entirely man-made — built in the 1850s–60s.

02 · Brooklyn Bridge · East River

Brooklyn Bridge

A gothic-arched icon over the East River, with one of the best skyline views in the city.

Fact To prove it was safe in 1883, they marched 21 elephants across it.

03 · Times Square · Midtown

Times Square

The city at full volume — billboards, yellow cabs, and endless crowds.

Fact Zoning here requires bright signs — dimness is against the rules.

04 · Statue of Liberty · New York Harbor

Statue of Liberty

A copper giant in the harbor since 1886, welcoming arrivals to New York.

Fact A gift from France — her green is a century of weathering.
